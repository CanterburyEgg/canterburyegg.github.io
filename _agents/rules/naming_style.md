# Naming & Dash Discipline

AI models overuse hyphens to make invented names sound "quirky" (e.g., "Rattle-Pick", "Grav-Iron", "D.U.S.T.-1N"). The user finds this a strong AI tell.

## Rules
- **Invented proper names (characters, items, places, tools, resources, factions) should default to NO hyphens.** Use a single compound word ("Rattlepick", "Graviron") or two plain words ("Thermal Drill", "Humming Basalt").
- A hyphen in a name is allowed only when it's genuinely the natural real-world form (e.g., "X-ray"). Budget: at most ~1 in 15 names. When in doubt, drop it.
- Avoid stylized acronym/serial-number names with punctuation (e.g., "D.U.S.T.-1N"). Prefer a plain name ("Dustin").
- In prose, dialogue, and docs, use em dashes and en dashes sparingly too. Prefer commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
- Before finalizing any batch of names or text, scan it for hyphens and dashes and justify each one that remains.

## Don't over-invent compound words either
- Fake compound words ("Rustcopper", "Graviron", "Glowquartz", "Voidtin") are the same AI tell as hyphens. Budget: at most ~1 invented compound per list of ~6 names.
- Prefer **real words** (Bismuth Ore, Basalt, Quartz) and get the alien or sci-fi flavor from a **plain adjective + real noun** ("Humming Basalt", "Glowing Quartz", "Coil Vine").
- **Follow RuneScape's material logic for gear**: tools and equipment are named `<Material> <Tool>` (Bismuth Pick, Cobalt Sickle), and better materials make better gear. Avoid the cliched bronze/iron/steel ladder; prefer uncommon real materials or a sparing invented one. Don't give tools whimsical standalone names ("Thermal Drill", "Arc Scythe").

## Avoid the "A, B, and funny C" list joke
- The three-item list where the last item is the punchline ("Rakes, a broken drone, and one very old boot") is a common AI writing tell. Avoid it by default.
- Get humor from a single specific detail, a reaction, or a two-beat setup and payoff instead ("A sign reads DO NOT TOUCH. A smaller sign under it reads THAT ONE EITHER.").
- Budget: at most one per quest or batch of flavor text, and never in two neighboring objects.
