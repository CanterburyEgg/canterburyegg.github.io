Overview & Win Conditions

A 2-player asymmetric street-warfare card game pitting a sprawling, resource-heavy Organization against a clandestine, agile Individual cell.

Organization

The Established Power

Controls institutional channels, deploys public defenses, and executes multi-stage Operations to achieve total domain dominance.

Win Condition: Complete Operations worth a total of 5 Victory Points.
Individual

The Resistance Cell

Operates from a street hideout, recruiting Operatives and executing precision Raids to dismantle corporate infrastructure.

Win Condition: Reach 5 Reputation (via successful Raids or the 10-Cash Free Action buy).

Turn Structure & Action Budgets

Players take alternating turns spending discrete action budgets on their main turn phase.

Organization

3 Actions Per Turn

  • Starting Cash: $5
  • Turn Order: Acts First
  • Turn 1 Restrictions: Cannot advance Operations to Stage 3 or play Edicts on Turn 1.
Individual

4 Actions Per Turn

  • Starting Cash: $3
  • Turn Order: Acts Second
  • Free Action: Spend 10 Cash → Gain 1 Reputation (Costs 0 actions).

Standard Turn Sequence

  1. Phase 1: Refresh / Upkeep — Reset active units/cards. Collect passive yields from active Infrastructure / Contacts. Trigger "Start of Turn" effects.
  2. Phase 2: Mandatory Draw — Draw 1 card from deck.
  3. Phase 3: Action Phase (Main) — Spend action budget (3 for Org, 4 for Individual) in any order on basic actions or card abilities:
    ActionOrganization (3 Actions)Individual (4 Actions)
    EconomyGain 1 Cash.Gain 1 Cash.
    DrawDraw 1 Card.Draw 1 Card.
    DeployPlay Enforcer, Infra, Operation, Directive.Play Operative, Contact, Tool.
    Advance / ExecuteAdvance Op ($Lv Cash + 1 Action) or play Edict.Play Tactic from hand.
    SpecialUse card ability.Launch Raid (Limit 1/turn) / Buy Rep (Free).
  4. Phase 4: Cleanup Phase — Discard down to max hand size limit (Standard: 5 cards). Pass turn.

The Operation & Advance Engine

Operations sit face-up in the Command Zone and act as ticking corporate threats driving the Org's victory path.

Lifecycle & Rules of Operations

  • Deployment Window: An Operation costs its printed base cost to play into the Command Zone. Operations cannot be advanced on the exact turn they are deployed.
  • Stage Progression (Stage 1 → Stage 2 → Stage 3):
    • Advance to Stage 2: Costs 1 Action + $2 Cash. Activates Stage 2 passive rules/hazards.
    • Advance to Stage 3 (Completion): Costs 1 Action + $3 Cash. Triggers Stage 3 Completion Effect.
  • Scoring Completion: Upon paying to complete Stage 3, the Operation triggers its completion rule, moves to the Org's Scored Zone, and awards its printed VP value (1, 2, or 3 VP). Average win requires ~3 completed Operations (e.g., 2+2+1 VP).
  • Raid Breach Loss: If an Individual successfully breaches an Operation at any stage, the Operation is destroyed, sent to the Org discard pile, and awards the Individual 1 Reputation.

The Raid & Combat Resolution Loop

Raids are direct, single-number comparison engagements launched by the Individual against Org channels, Operations, or HQ.

Step-by-Step Raid Execution

  1. Declaration: Individual spends 1 Action (Limit 1 Raid/turn) to select a target (Operation in Command Zone, Channel Infrastructure, or Central Treasury HQ) and commits active field Operatives from the Street Zone.
  2. Sequential Alignment: Committed Operatives line up against face-up Enforcers guarding that lane/target. Engagements are resolved strictly 1-on-1 in defender sequence.
  3. Hidden Information Window:
    • Organization: May pay cash cost to flip face-down Directives attached to the active Enforcer.
    • Individual: May play instant Tactics from hand or trigger active Tool abilities.
  4. Single-Number Comparison: Compare total modified Operative Attack Rating vs. Enforcer Defense Rating:
    • Operative Victory (Attack ≥ Defense): Enforcer is defeated and sent to Org discard pile. Operative proceeds.
    • Enforcer Victory (Defense > Attack): Operative is defeated and sent directly to the Individual Discard Pile (NO WOUND TRACKERS / PERMADEATH).
  5. Bail Window: Between defender line engagements, the Individual may choose to retreat (bail out) with surviving Operatives. Once an engagement begins, it is a point-of-no-return.
  6. Breach Payout: If Operatives pass all defenders and breach the target:
    • Targeting Operation: Operation is destroyed → Individual gains 1 Reputation.
    • Targeting HQ: Individual accesses central reserves (steals cash / views hand).

Factions & World Identities

A completely privatized world featuring 4 Corporate Organizations and 4 Underground Rebel Movements.

Organization Factions

Finance Conglomerate

Market dominance, liquidity, high cash generation, and massive financial leverage.

Religious Cult / Church

Zealotry, tithes, attrition, and devastating martyrdom effects when defenders fall.

Bio-Tech Lab / Pharm Giant

Mutagenic evolution, asset recycling from discard, and adaptive biological defenses.

Propaganda Machine

Public smearing, hidden directives, psychological warfare, and narrative manipulation.

Individual Factions

Syndicate / Ghost Cell

Black-market brokers and shadow intelligence. High cash, deep espionage, stealth.

Populists

Mass movement & labor union. Swarming cheap operatives, choking corporate actions.

Street Artificers

Junkyard survivalists. Scavenging discard pile, improvising combat gear, hardware mods.

Media Pirates

Truth broadcasters & whistleblowers. Exposing corporate crime, stripping VP, broadcast Raids.

Deckbuilding Constraints & Setup

Standardized parameters for tournament and constructed play.

  • Main Deck Minimum: Exactly 45 Cards minimum.
  • Card Copy Limit: Maximum 3 Copies of any unique card per deck.
  • Identity Card: Exactly 1 Identity Card (does not count toward 45-card main deck).
  • Faction Composition: Decks may include neutral cards and cards matching the selected Identity's Faction tag.
  • First Matchup Composition (15 x 3): A complete main deck is made of 15 unique card designs running 3 copies each.
